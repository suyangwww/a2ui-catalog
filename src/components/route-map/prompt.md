An interactive cartographic route map Component with category filtering, street-style connecting route polylines, tappable landmark pins, detour notes, and an Expand modal/bottom-sheet. Use RouteMap for city sightseeing routes, multi-day road trips, or neighborhood guides.
Platform behavior: Pins expand to >= 44px touch targets on mobile, and tapping Expand opens a draggable Bottom Sheet on iPhone.

Props: `title`, `subtitle`, `regionPreset`, `value`, `categoryFilter`, `stops`, `expandable`, `height`, `weight`
