An interactive data-driven cartographic route map Component with automatic `(lat, lng)` bounding-box projection, optional declarative backdrop `features`, category filtering, connecting route polylines, tappable numbered pins, detour notes, and an Expand modal/bottom-sheet. Use RouteMap for walking itineraries, delivery routes, multi-day road trips, or neighborhood guides.
Platform behavior: Pins expand to >= 44px touch targets on mobile, and tapping Expand opens a draggable Bottom Sheet on iPhone.

Props: `title`, `subtitle`, `value`, `categoryFilter`, `bounds`, `features`, `routeStyle`, `stops`, `expandable`, `height`, `weight`
