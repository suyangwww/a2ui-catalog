An interactive itemized expense and bill splitter Component with editable participant names, dynamic add/remove line items, per-item multi-participant assignment, `$ / %` tax and gratuity controls, and cent-exact largest-remainder allocation. Use SplitLedger for group travel expenses, team offsites, household splits, or restaurant receipts (`card` surface or `clipboard` paper).
Platform behavior: Stacks item inputs and preset percentage pills cleanly on mobile with >= 44px touch controls and one-tap clipboard export.
