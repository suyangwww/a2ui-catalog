An interactive itemized bill and shared-expense splitter Component with editable guest names, dynamic add/remove line items, per-item multi-guest assignment, `$ / %` tax and tip controls, and cent-exact largest-remainder allocation. Use SplitLedger for restaurant receipts, group travel expenses, or household splits (`clipboard` paper or `card` surface).
Platform behavior: Stacks item inputs and preset tip pills cleanly on mobile with >= 44px touch controls and one-tap clipboard export.
