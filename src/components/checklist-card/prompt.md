An interactive checkable step and task Component with a live `X of N` completion counter, progress bar, timestamps, and strikethrough physics. Use ChecklistCard for cooking schedules, travel packing lists, assembly guides, or preparation checklists.
Platform behavior: Every item row enforces a >= 44px touch target on iPad and iPhone with smooth spring completion feedback.
